📋 Harvesting Tips

  • 📌 Harvest when grain moisture is 15-18%
  • 🌾 Cut early morning (8-11 AM) for better quality
  • 🏠 Dry rice to 14% moisture before storage
  • 📦 Store in cool, dry place away from moisture
  • 🐁 Use airtight containers to prevent pest damage
